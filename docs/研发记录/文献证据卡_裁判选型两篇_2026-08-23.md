## 论文 1：On scalable oversight with weak LLMs judging strong LLMs

- 作者/机构：Zachary Kenton, Noah Y. Siegel 等，Google DeepMind；
- 来源：arXiv:2407.04622v2（2024-07-12），NeurIPS 2024 会议版同内容；
- PDF SHA-256：`ff0a323d6109044e…`（完整值见 tmp/weak_judges.sha）；54 页；提取文本 110,012 字符。

| # | 证据卡 |
|---|---|
| W1 | **claim**：弱裁判-强辩手设置下，辩论协议 vs 咨询协议的效果取决于任务类型。**quote**：摘要 "Comparing debate to direct question answering, the results depend on the type of task: in extractive QA tasks with information asymmetry debate outperforms direct question answering, but in other tasks without information asymmetry the results are mixed."（提取文本 char 330–560，§1 摘要段）。**type**=direct；**stance**=support；**confidence**=high；**conditions**=LLM 扮演裁判与辩手、无真人；**human_review_required**=true |

**对本项目的直接含义**（读者推断，非论文结论）："弱模型当裁判"只有在有信息不对称的抽取类任务上才可能可靠；我们的裁决任务是"证据充分性判定"（近似信息不对称场景），可以用小模型裁判做**初步筛选**，但**不能把弱裁判当最终裁决**——凡争议样本必须升级强模型或人工。此推断需在我们自己的 390 条数据上验证（维度 4 实验）。

## 论文 2：Tuning LLM Judge Design Decisions for 1/1000 of the Cost

- 作者/机构：David Salinas, Omar Swelam, Frank Hutter，弗莱堡大学 + ELLIS Tübingen；
- 来源：arXiv:2501.17178；ICML 2025（PMLR 267）；
- PDF SHA-256：`e7d2435a6c029adb…`（完整值见 tmp/judge_design.sha）；17 页；提取文本 62,114 字符。

| # | 证据卡 |
|---|---|
| J1 | **claim**：评估一个 LLM 裁判配置很贵，且不同论文之间模型/prompt/超参同时变化，无法公平比较。**quote**："evaluating one model in Alpaca-Eval costs ∼24$…Given this, many confounding factors are typically present between different judges approaches"（char≈2,200–2,900，§1；行 52–58）。**type**=direct；**stance**=support；**confidence**=high；**conditions**=Alpaca-Eval 定价语境；**human_review_required**=true |
| J2 | **claim**：用多目标多保真搜索调裁判超参（prompt 设计 + 基座模型），可同时优化准确率与成本，并把搜索本身的花费压到可行范围。**quote**："we propose to leverage multi-objective multi-fidelity which allows to find judges that trade accuracy for cost and also significantly reduce the cost of the search"（摘要 char 700–830；方法 §3 与附录 Pareto 段 char≈86,000 附近）。**type**=direct；**stance**=support；**confidence**=high；**conditions**=开源权重模型池；**human_review_required**=true |
| J3 | **claim**：找到的裁判（开源权重模型）在不同预算约束下匹配或超过此前（贵得多）的裁判配置。**quote**："The judge we found match or outperforms the judge considered at much lower cost. Importantly, the judge configurations are open-weight models…"（char≈50,500 附近，§5 结果段；行 788–791）。**type**=direct；**stance**=support；**confidence**=high；**conditions**=与 GPT-4-1106-preview 级裁判对比；**human_review_required**=true |
| J4 | **claim**：按准确率+成本选出的裁判没有加剧位置偏差，但风格/冗长等其他偏差可能被该选型方法放大。**quote**："we measured the flip rate…found a strong negative correlation with the human agreement scores (r = −0.789)…other biases, such as stylistic preferences or verbosity, may be worsen by our method"（§6 Limitations，行 797–806）。**type**=direct；**stance**=support；**confidence**=high；**conditions**=top-rung 配置；**human_review_required**=true |
| J5 | **claim**：该方法的优化目标只有准确率与成本，未包含稳定性与可解释性。**quote**："our multi-objective procedure could be extended to optimize for additional criteria such as stability or explain-ability"（§7 Conclusion，行 818–820）。**type**=direct；**stance**=support；**confidence**=high；**conditions**=作者自述局限；**human_review_required**=true |

**对本项目的直接含义**（读者推断）：我们 R009（校准器）与模型选型矩阵应当采用同样的"多保真"策略——先在 8 条冒烟上粗筛裁判配置，再在 390 条上精比，而不是每换一个配置就全量跑一遍；并把"位置偏差/自偏好"作为选型时的附加否决项（与维度 5 一致）。
