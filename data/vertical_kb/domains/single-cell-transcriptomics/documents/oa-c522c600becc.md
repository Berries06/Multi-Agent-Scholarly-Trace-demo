# Chromatin architecture reorganization during stem cell differentiation

## Method
Higher-order chromatin structure is emerging as an important regulator of gene expression.

## Evaluation
By mapping genome-wide chromatin interactions in human embryonic stem (ES) cells and four human ES-cell-derived lineages, we uncover extensive chromatin reorganization during lineage specification.
Our results therefore provide a global view of chromatin dynamics and a resource for studying long-range control of gene expression in distinct human cell lineages.

## Finding
Higher-order chromatin structure is emerging as an important regulator of gene expression.
We observe that although self-associating chromatin domains are stable during differentiation, chromatin interactions both within and between domains change in a striking manner, altering 36% of active and inactive.
