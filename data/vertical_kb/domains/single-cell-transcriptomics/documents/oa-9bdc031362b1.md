# Fly Cell Atlas: A single-nucleus transcriptomic atlas of the adult fruit fly

## Method
For more than 100 years, the fruit fly Drosophila melanogaster has been one of the most studied model organisms.
Here, we present a single-cell atlas of the adult fly, Tabula Drosophilae , that includes 580,000 nuclei from 15 individually dissected sexed tissues as well as the entire head and body, annotated to >250 distinct cell.

## Evaluation
Here, we present a single-cell atlas of the adult fly, Tabula Drosophilae , that includes 580,000 nuclei from 15 individually dissected sexed tissues as well as the entire head and body, annotated to >250 distinct cell.
We provide an in-depth analysis of cell type–related gene signatures and transcription factor markers, as well as sexual dimorphism, across the whole animal.

## Finding
Analysis of common cell types between tissues, such as blood and muscle cells, reveals rare cell types and tissue-specific subtypes.
