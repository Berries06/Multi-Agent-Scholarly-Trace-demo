# MetaboAnalyst 4.0: towards more transparent and integrative metabolomics analysis

## Method
We present a new update to MetaboAnalyst (version 4.0) for comprehensive metabolomic data analysis, interpretation, and integration with other omics data.
Since the last major update in 2015, MetaboAnalyst has continued to evolve based on user feedback and technological advancements in the field.

## Evaluation
The user interface of MetaboAnalyst 4.0 has been reengineered to provide a more modern look and feel, as well as to give more space and flexibility to introduce new functions.

## Finding
MetaboAnalyst 4.0 is freely available at http://metaboanalyst.ca.
