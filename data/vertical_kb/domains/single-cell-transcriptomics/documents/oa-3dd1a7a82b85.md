# Giotto: a toolbox for integrative analysis and visualization of spatial expression data

## Method
Here we present Giotto, a comprehensive and open-source toolbox for spatial data analysis and visualization.

## Evaluation
Spatial transcriptomic and proteomic technologies have provided new opportunities to investigate cells in their native microenvironment.
The analysis module provides end-to-end analysis by implementing a wide range of algorithms for characterizing tissue composition, spatial expression patterns, and cellular interactions.

## Finding
To demonstrate its general applicability, we apply Giotto to a wide range of datasets encompassing diverse technologies and platforms.
