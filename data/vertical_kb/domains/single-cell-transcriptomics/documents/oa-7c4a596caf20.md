# De novo assembly of bacterial transcriptomes from RNA-seq data

## Method
Here, we present novel algorithms, specific to bacterial gene structures and transcriptomes, for analysis of bacterial RNA-seq data and de novo transcriptome assembly.

## Evaluation
The algorithms are implemented in an open source software system called Rockhopper 2.

## Finding
We find that Rockhopper 2 outperforms other de novo transcriptome assemblers and offers accurate and efficient analysis of bacterial RNA-seq data.
