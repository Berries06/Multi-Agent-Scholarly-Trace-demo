# DeepCpG: accurate prediction of single-cell DNA methylation states using deep learning

## Method
We report DeepCpG, a computational approach based on deep neural networks to predict methylation states in single cells.
Additionally, we show that the model parameters can be interpreted, thereby providing insights into how sequence composition affects methylation variability.

## Evaluation
Recent technological advances have enabled DNA methylation to be assayed at single-cell resolution.
We report DeepCpG, a computational approach based on deep neural networks to predict methylation states in single cells.

## Finding
However, current protocols are limited by incomplete CpG coverage and hence methods to predict missing methylation states are critical to enable genome-wide analyses.
Additionally, we show that the model parameters can be interpreted, thereby providing insights into how sequence composition affects methylation variability.
