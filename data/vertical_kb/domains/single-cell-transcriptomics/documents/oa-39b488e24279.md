# SRAMP: prediction of mammalian N 6 -methyladenosine (m 6 A) sites based on sequence-derived features

## Method
SRAMP uses either genomic sequences or cDNA sequences as its input.

## Evaluation
High-throughput experiments have demonstrated that only a small fraction of the m(6)A consensus motifs in mammalian transcriptomes are modified.

## Finding
With either kind of input sequence, SRAMP achieves competitive performance in both cross-validation tests and rigorous independent benchmarking tests.
