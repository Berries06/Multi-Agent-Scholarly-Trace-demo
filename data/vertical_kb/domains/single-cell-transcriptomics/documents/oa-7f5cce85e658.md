# Confronting false discoveries in single-cell differential expression

## Method
Differential expression analysis in single-cell transcriptomics enables the dissection of cell-type-specific responses to perturbations such as disease, trauma, or experimental manipulations.

## Evaluation
Differential expression analysis in single-cell transcriptomics enables the dissection of cell-type-specific responses to perturbations such as disease, trauma, or experimental manipulations.

## Finding
Differential expression analysis in single-cell transcriptomics enables the dissection of cell-type-specific responses to perturbations such as disease, trauma, or experimental manipulations.
Here, we show that the relative performance of these methods is contingent on their ability to account for variation between biological replicates.
