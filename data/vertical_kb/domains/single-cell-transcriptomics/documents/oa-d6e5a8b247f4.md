# BBKNN: fast batch alignment of single cell transcriptomes

## Method
To overcome the challenge of enormous datasets, we have developed BBKNN, an extremely fast graph-based data integration algorithm.

## Evaluation
MOTIVATION: Increasing numbers of large scale single cell RNA-Seq projects are leading to a data explosion, which can only be fully exploited through data integration.
We illustrate the power of BBKNN on large scale mouse atlasing data, and favourably benchmark its run time against a number of competing methods.
