# Single-cell atlas reveals correlates of high cognitive function, dementia, and resilience to Alzheimer’s disease pathology

## Method
Alzheimer's disease (AD) is the most common cause of dementia worldwide, but the molecular and cellular mechanisms underlying cognitive impairment remain poorly understood.

## Evaluation
To address this, we generated a single-cell transcriptomic atlas of the aged human prefrontal cortex covering 2.3 million cells from postmortem human brain samples of 427 individuals with varying degrees of AD pathology.
