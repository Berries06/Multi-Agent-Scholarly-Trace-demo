# Single-cell and spatial analysis reveal interaction of FAP+ fibroblasts and SPP1+ macrophages in colorectal cancer

## Method
Abstract Colorectal cancer (CRC) is among the most common malignancies with limited treatments other than surgery.

## Evaluation
The tumor microenvironment (TME) profiling enables the discovery of potential therapeutic targets.
Here, we profile 54,103 cells from tumor and adjacent tissues to characterize cellular composition and elucidate the potential origin and regulation of tumor-enriched cell types in CRC.
