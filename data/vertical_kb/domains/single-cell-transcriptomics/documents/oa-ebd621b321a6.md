# DeepLoc: prediction of protein subcellular localization using deep learning

## Method
RESULTS: Here, we present a prediction algorithm using deep neural networks to predict protein subcellular localization relying only on sequence information.
At its core, the prediction model uses a recurrent neural network that processes the entire protein sequence and an attention mechanism identifying protein regions important for the subcellular localization.

## Evaluation
The model was trained and tested on a protein dataset extracted from one of the latest UniProt releases, in which experimentally annotated proteins follow more stringent criteria than previously.
The dataset is available at http://www.cbs.dtu.dk/services/DeepLoc/data.php.

## Finding
RESULTS: Here, we present a prediction algorithm using deep neural networks to predict protein subcellular localization relying only on sequence information.
We demonstrate that our model achieves a good accuracy (78% for 10 categories; 92% for membrane-bound or soluble), outperforming current state-of-the-art algorithms, including those relying on homology information.
