# Systematic pan-cancer analysis of tumour purity

## Method
Here we present a systematic analysis using different measurement modalities of tumour purity in >10,000 samples across 21 cancer types from the Cancer Genome Atlas.
Finally, using a differential expression method that accounts for tumour purity, we find an immunotherapy gene signature in several cancer types that is not detected by traditional differential expression analyses.

## Evaluation
The tumour microenvironment is the non-cancerous cells present in and around a tumour, including mainly immune cells, but also fibroblasts and cells that comprise supporting blood vessels.
These non-cancerous components of the tumour may play an important role in cancer biology.

## Finding
They also have a strong influence on the genomic analysis of tumour samples, and may alter the biological interpretation of results.
We demonstrate the confounding effect of tumour purity on correlating and clustering tumours with transcriptomics data.
