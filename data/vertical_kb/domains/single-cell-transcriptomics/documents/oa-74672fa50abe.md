# Alveolar regeneration through a Krt8+ transitional stem cell state that persists in human lung fibrosis

## Method
Using time-series single cell RNA-seq of the bleomycin lung injury model, we resolved transcriptional dynamics for 28 cell types.
We generated a model of gene regulatory programs leading to Krt8+ transitional cells and their terminal differentiation to alveolar type-1 cells.

## Evaluation
The cell type specific sequences of transcriptional programs during lung regeneration have remained elusive.
Using time-series single cell RNA-seq of the bleomycin lung injury model, we resolved transcriptional dynamics for 28 cell types.

## Finding
We propose that in lung fibrosis, perturbed molecular checkpoints on the way to terminal differentiation can cause aberrant persistence of regenerative intermediate stem cell states.
