# Single cell RNA sequencing of human microglia uncovers a subset associated with Alzheimer’s disease

## Method
The extent of microglial heterogeneity in humans remains a central yet poorly explored question in light of the development of therapies targeting this cell type.

## Evaluation
The extent of microglial heterogeneity in humans remains a central yet poorly explored question in light of the development of therapies targeting this cell type.
Here, we investigate the population structure of live microglia purified from human cerebral cortex samples obtained at autopsy and during neurosurgical procedures.

## Finding
Using single cell RNA sequencing, we find that some subsets are enriched for disease-related genes and RNA signatures.
