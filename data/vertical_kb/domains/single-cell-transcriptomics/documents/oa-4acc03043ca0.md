# A single-cell atlas of the multicellular ecosystem of primary and metastatic hepatocellular carcinoma

## Method
Abstract Hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) represents a paradigm of the relation between tumor microenvironment (TME) and tumor development.

## Evaluation
Abstract Hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) represents a paradigm of the relation between tumor microenvironment (TME) and tumor development.
Here, we generate a single-cell atlas of the multicellular ecosystem of HCC from four tissue sites.

## Finding
We show the enrichment of central memory T cells (T CM ) in the early tertiary lymphoid structures (E-TLSs) in HCC and assess the relationships between chronic HBV/HCV infection and T cell infiltration and exhaustion.
We find the MMP9 + macrophages to be terminally differentiated tumor-associated macrophages (TAMs) and PPARγ to be the pivotal transcription factor driving their differentiation.
