# Shared molecular neuropathology across major psychiatric disorders parallels polygenic overlap

## Method
The predisposition to neuropsychiatric disease involves a complex, polygenic, and pleiotropic genetic architecture.
This comprehensive systems-level view of the neurobiological architecture of major neuropsychiatric illness demonstrates pathways of molecular convergence and specificity.

## Evaluation
We identified patterns of shared and distinct gene-expression perturbations across these conditions.

## Finding
This comprehensive systems-level view of the neurobiological architecture of major neuropsychiatric illness demonstrates pathways of molecular convergence and specificity.
