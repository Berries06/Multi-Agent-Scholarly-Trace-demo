# CUT&Tag for efficient epigenomic profiling of small samples and single cells

## Method
All steps from live cells to sequencing-ready libraries can be performed in a single tube on the benchtop or a microwell in a high-throughput pipeline, and the entire procedure can be performed in one day.

## Evaluation
A complete understanding of gene regulation will require the mapping of specific chromatin features in small samples of cells at high resolution.
All steps from live cells to sequencing-ready libraries can be performed in a single tube on the benchtop or a microwell in a high-throughput pipeline, and the entire procedure can be performed in one day.

## Finding
We demonstrate the utility of CUT&Tag by profiling histone modifications, RNA Polymerase II and transcription factors on low cell numbers and single cells.
