# scMC learns biological variation through the alignment of multiple single-cell genomics datasets

## Method
Here, we present an integration method scMC to remove the technical variation while preserving the intrinsic biological variation.

## Evaluation
Distinguishing biological from technical variation is crucial when integrating and comparing single-cell genomics datasets across different experiments.
Application of scMC to both simulated and real datasets from single-cell RNA-seq and ATAC-seq experiments demonstrates its capability of detecting context-shared and context-specific biological signals via accurate.

## Finding
Application of scMC to both simulated and real datasets from single-cell RNA-seq and ATAC-seq experiments demonstrates its capability of detecting context-shared and context-specific biological signals via accurate.
