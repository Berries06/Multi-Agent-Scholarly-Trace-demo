# Collagen-producing lung cell atlas identifies multiple subsets with distinct localization and relevance to fibrosis

## Method
Collagen-producing cells maintain the complex architecture of the lung and drive pathologic scarring in pulmonary fibrosis.

## Evaluation
Collagen-producing cells maintain the complex architecture of the lung and drive pathologic scarring in pulmonary fibrosis.
Here we perform single-cell RNA-sequencing to identify all collagen-producing cells in normal and fibrotic lungs.

## Finding
Our atlas of collagen-producing cells provides a roadmap for studying the roles of these unique populations in homeostasis and pathologic fibrosis.
