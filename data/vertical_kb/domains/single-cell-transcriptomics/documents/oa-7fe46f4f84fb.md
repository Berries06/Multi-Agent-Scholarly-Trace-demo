# Eleven grand challenges in single-cell data science

## Method

## Evaluation
The recent boom in microfluidics and combinatorial indexing strategies, combined with low sequencing costs, has empowered single-cell sequencing technology.
Thousands-or even millions-of cells analyzed in a single experiment amount to a data revolution in single-cell biology and pose unique data science problems.

## Finding
This compendium is for established researchers, newcomers, and students alike, highlighting interesting and rewarding problems for the coming years.
