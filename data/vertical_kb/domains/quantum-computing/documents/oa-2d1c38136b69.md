# Towards practical quantum computers: transmon qubit with a lifetime approaching 0.5 milliseconds

## Method
Abstract Here we report a breakthrough in the fabrication of a long lifetime transmon qubit.
We use tantalum films as the base superconductor.

## Evaluation
Abstract Here we report a breakthrough in the fabrication of a long lifetime transmon qubit.
By using a dry etching process, we obtained transmon qubits with a best T1 lifetime of 503 μs.

## Finding
As a result, the current breakthrough indicates that the dry etching process of tantalum film is a promising approach to fabricate medium- or large-scale superconducting quantum circuits with a much longer lifetime.
