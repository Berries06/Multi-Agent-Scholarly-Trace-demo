# Quantum circuit learning

## Method
We propose a classical-quantum hybrid algorithm for machine learning on near-term quantum processors, which we call quantum circuit learning.
A quantum circuit driven by our framework learns a given task by tuning parameters implemented on it.

## Evaluation
The iterative optimization of the parameters allows us to circumvent the high-depth circuit.

## Finding
Hybridizing a low-depth quantum circuit and a classical computer for machine learning, the proposed framework paves the way toward applications of near-term quantum devices for quantum machine learning.
