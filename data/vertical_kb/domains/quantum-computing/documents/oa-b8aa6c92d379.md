# A Race-Track Trapped-Ion Quantum Processor

## Method
We describe and benchmark a new quantum charge-coupled device (QCCD) trapped-ion quantum computer based on a linear trap with periodic boundary conditions, which resembles a race track.

## Evaluation
We describe and benchmark a new quantum charge-coupled device (QCCD) trapped-ion quantum computer based on a linear trap with periodic boundary conditions, which resembles a race track.
The system is initially operated with 32 qubits, but future upgrades will allow for more.

## Finding
We also discuss future upgrades to the new system aimed at adding more qubits and capabilities.
