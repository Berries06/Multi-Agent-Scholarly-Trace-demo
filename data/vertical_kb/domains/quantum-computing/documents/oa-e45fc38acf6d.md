# Building logical qubits in a superconducting quantum computing system

## Method
Abstract The technological world is in the midst of a quantum computing and quantum information revolution.

## Evaluation
We believe that the next significant step will be to demonstrate a quantum memory, in which a system of interacting qubits stores an encoded logical qubit state longer than the incorporated parts.
Here, we describe the important route towards a logical memory with superconducting qubits, employing a rotated version of the surface code.

## Finding
We believe that the next significant step will be to demonstrate a quantum memory, in which a system of interacting qubits stores an encoded logical qubit state longer than the incorporated parts.
The current status of technology with regards to interconnected superconducting-qubit networks will be described and near-term areas of focus to improve devices will be identified.
