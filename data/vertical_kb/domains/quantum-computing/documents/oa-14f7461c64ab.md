# Cost function dependent barren plateaus in shallow parametrized quantum circuits

## Method
Variational quantum algorithms (VQAs) optimize the parameters θ of a parametrized quantum circuit V(θ) to minimize a cost function C.

## Evaluation
We illustrate these ideas with large-scale simulations, up to 100 qubits, of a quantum autoencoder implementation.

## Finding
While VQAs may enable practical applications of noisy quantum computers, they are nevertheless heuristic methods with unproven scaling.
Here, we rigorously prove two results, assuming V(θ) is an alternating layered ansatz composed of blocks forming local 2-designs.
