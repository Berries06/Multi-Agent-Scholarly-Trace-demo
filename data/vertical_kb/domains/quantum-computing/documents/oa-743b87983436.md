# High-Fidelity Control and Entanglement of Rydberg-Atom Qubits

## Method
We report progress towards high-fidelity quantum control of Rydberg-atom qubits.
Enabled by a reduction in laser phase noise, our approach yields a significant improvement in coherence properties of individual qubits.

## Evaluation
Individual neutral atoms excited to Rydberg states are a promising platform for quantum simulation and quantum information processing.
We report progress towards high-fidelity quantum control of Rydberg-atom qubits.
