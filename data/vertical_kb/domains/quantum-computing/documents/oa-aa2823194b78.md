# Training deep quantum neural networks

## Method
Here we propose a truly quantum analogue of classical neurons, which form quantum feedforward neural networks capable of universal quantum computation.
Our method allows for fast optimisation with reduced memory requirements: the number of qudits required scales with only the width, allowing deep-network optimisation.

## Evaluation
We benchmark our proposal for the quantum task of learning an unknown unitary and find remarkable generalisation behaviour and a striking robustness to noisy training data.

## Finding
We describe the efficient training of these networks using the fidelity as a cost function, providing both classical and efficient quantum implementations.
