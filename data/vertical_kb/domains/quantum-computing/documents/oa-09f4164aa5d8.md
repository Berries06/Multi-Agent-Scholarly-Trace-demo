# Evidence for the utility of quantum computing before fault tolerance

## Method
Here we report experiments on a noisy 127-qubit processor and demonstrate the measurement of accurate expectation values for circuit volumes at a scale beyond brute-force classical computation.
These experiments demonstrate a foundational tool for the realization of near-term quantum applications 4,5 .

## Evaluation
Here we report experiments on a noisy 127-qubit processor and demonstrate the measurement of accurate expectation values for circuit volumes at a scale beyond brute-force classical computation.
We establish the accuracy of the measured expectation values by comparing them with the output of exactly verifiable circuits.
