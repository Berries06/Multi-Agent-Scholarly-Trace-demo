# Two-qubit silicon quantum processor with operation fidelity exceeding 99%

## Method
We use a two-qubit Si/SiGe quantum processor to demonstrate state preparation and readout with fidelity greater than 97%, combined with both single- and two-qubit control fidelities exceeding 99%.

## Evaluation
Silicon spin qubits satisfy the necessary criteria for quantum information processing.
However, a demonstration of high-fidelity state preparation and readout combined with high-fidelity single- and two-qubit gates, all of which must be present for quantum error correction, has been lacking.

## Finding
