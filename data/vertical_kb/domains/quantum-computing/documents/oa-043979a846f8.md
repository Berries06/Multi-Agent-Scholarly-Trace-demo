# Milestones Toward Majorana-Based Quantum Computing

## Method
Preparing, manipulating, and reading out Majorana zero modes is important for quantum computing.

## Evaluation
A theoretically developed set of milestone experiments, if conducted successfully, may pave the way for fault-tolerant ``topological'' quantum information processing.
