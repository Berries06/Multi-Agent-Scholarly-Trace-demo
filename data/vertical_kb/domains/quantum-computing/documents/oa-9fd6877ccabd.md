# Logical quantum processor based on reconfigurable atom arrays

## Method
Here we report the realization of a programmable quantum processor based on encoded logical qubits operating with up to 280 physical qubits.
Using logical-level control and a zoned architecture in reconfigurable neutral-atom arrays 7 , our system combines high two-qubit gate fidelities 8 , arbitrary connectivity 7,9 , as well as fully programmable.
