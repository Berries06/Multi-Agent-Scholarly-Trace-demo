# Digital quantum simulation of fermionic models with a superconducting circuit

## Method
Here we use digital methods to construct the required arbitrary interactions, and perform quantum simulation of up to four fermionic modes with a superconducting quantum circuit.
We employ in excess of 300 quantum logic gates, and reach fidelities that are consistent with a simple model of uncorrelated errors.

## Evaluation

## Finding
The presented approach is in principle scalable to a larger number of modes, and arbitrary spatial dimensions.
