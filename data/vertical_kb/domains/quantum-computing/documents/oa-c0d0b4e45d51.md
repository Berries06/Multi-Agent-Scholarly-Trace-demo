# Training Variational Quantum Algorithms Is NP-Hard

## Method
They are based on the idea of using a classical computer to train a parametrized quantum circuit.
Moreover, the hardness is robust in the sense that, for every polynomial time algorithm, there are instances for which the relative error resulting from the classical optimization problem can be arbitrarily large.

## Evaluation
Even for classically tractable systems composed of only logarithmically many qubits or free fermions, we show the optimization to be NP-hard.

## Finding
We show that the corresponding classical optimization problems are NP-hard.
