# Connecting Ansatz Expressibility to Gradient Magnitudes and Barren Plateaus

## Method
Parametrized quantum circuits serve as ansatze for solving variational problems and provide a flexible paradigm for the programming of near-term quantum computers.

## Evaluation
Specifically, we calculate the variance in the cost gradient in terms of the expressibility of the ansatz, as measured by its distance from being a 2-design.

## Finding
Furthermore, we provide numerics illustrating the effect of expressibility on gradient scalings and we discuss the implications for designing strategies to avoid barren plateaus.
