# Quantum repeaters: From quantum networks to the quantum internet

## Method
Quantum technology is now at a point where practical work can begin on creating the quantum internet.

## Evaluation
A global-scale quantum internet requires the development of the quantum repeater, a device that stores and manipulates qubits while interacting with or emitting entangled photons.

## Finding
This review examines different approaches to quantum repeaters and networks, covering their conceptual frameworks, architectures, and current progress in experimental implementation.
