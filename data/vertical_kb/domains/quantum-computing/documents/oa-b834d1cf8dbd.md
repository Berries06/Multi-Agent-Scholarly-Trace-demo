# Time-crystalline eigenstate order on a quantum processor

## Method
Our work employs a time-reversal protocol to quantify the impact of external decoherence, and leverages quantum typicality to circumvent the exponential cost of densely sampling the eigenspectrum.
These results establish a scalable approach to studying non-equilibrium phases of matter on quantum processors.

## Evaluation
Here we implement tunable controlled-phase (CPHASE) gates on an array of superconducting qubits to experimentally observe an MBL-DTC and demonstrate its characteristic spatiotemporal response for generic initial.
