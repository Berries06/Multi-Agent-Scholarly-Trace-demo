# Variational ansatz-based quantum simulation of imaginary time evolution

## Method
Abstract Imaginary time evolution is a powerful tool for studying quantum systems.
We propose a variational algorithm for simulating imaginary time evolution on a hybrid quantum computer.

## Evaluation
We propose a variational algorithm for simulating imaginary time evolution on a hybrid quantum computer.

## Finding
As our algorithm is hybrid, suitable for error mitigation and can exploit shallow quantum circuits, it can be implemented with current quantum computers.
