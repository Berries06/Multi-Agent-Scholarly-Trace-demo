# Interfacing spin qubits in quantum dots and donors—hot, dense, and coherent

## Method
Excellent scalability is often argued for spin qubits defined by lithography and controlled via electrical signals, based on the success of conventional semiconductor integrated circuits.
Here, we review several strategies that are considered to address this crucial challenge in scaling quantum circuits based on electron spin qubits.
