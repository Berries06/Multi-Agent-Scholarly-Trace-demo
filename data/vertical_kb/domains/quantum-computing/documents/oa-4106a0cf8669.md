# Demonstration of qubit operations below a rigorous fault tolerance threshold with gate set tomography

## Method
Here we use gate set tomography to completely characterize operations on a trapped-Yb + -ion qubit and demonstrate with greater than 95% confidence that they satisfy a rigorous threshold for FTQEC (diamond norm ≤6.7 ×.

## Evaluation
But since qubits are noisy and error-prone, they will depend on fault-tolerant quantum error correction (FTQEC) to compute reliably.
Quantum error correction can protect against general noise if—and only if—the error in each physical qubit operation is smaller than a certain threshold.
