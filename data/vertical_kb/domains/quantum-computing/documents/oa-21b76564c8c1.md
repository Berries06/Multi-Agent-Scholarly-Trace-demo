# New material platform for superconducting transmon qubits with coherence times exceeding 0.3 milliseconds

## Method
Building large, useful quantum systems based on transmon qubits will require significant improvements in qubit relaxation and coherence times, which are orders of magnitude shorter than limits imposed by bulk properties.

## Evaluation
The superconducting transmon qubit is a leading platform for quantum computing and quantum science.

## Finding
