# High-threshold and low-overhead fault-tolerant quantum memory

## Method
Quantum error correction becomes practically realizable once the physical error rate is below a threshold value that depends on the choice of quantum code, syndrome measurement circuit and decoding algorithm 5 .
We present an end-to-end quantum error correction protocol that implements fault-tolerant memory on the basis of a family of low-density parity-check codes 6 .

## Evaluation
