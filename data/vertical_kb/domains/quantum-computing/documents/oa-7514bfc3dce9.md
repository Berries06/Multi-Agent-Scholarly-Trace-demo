# Quantum walks on a programmable two-dimensional 62-qubit superconducting processor

## Method
Quantum walks are the quantum mechanical analog of classical random walks and an extremely powerful tool in quantum simulations, quantum search algorithms, and even for universal quantum computing.

## Evaluation
In our work, we have designed and fabricated an 8-by-8 two-dimensional square superconducting qubit array composed of 62 functional qubits.

## Finding
We used this device to demonstrate high-fidelity single- and two-particle quantum walks.
