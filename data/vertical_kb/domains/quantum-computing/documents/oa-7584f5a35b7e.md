# Noisy intermediate-scale quantum algorithms

## Method
Noisy quantum computers can in principle perform reliable quantum computations, but truly scalable systems require noise levels lower than are presently achieved.

## Evaluation
Topic areas include the simulation of many-body physics and chemistry, combinatorial optimization, and machine learning.

## Finding
It is evident that the NISQ era has produced new paradigms for programming that will be built upon as quantum computers are further perfected.
