# Quantum Simulators: Architectures and Opportunities

## Method
Quantum simulators are a promising technology on the spectrum of quantum devices from specialized quantum experiments to universal quantum computers.

## Evaluation
Quantum simulators are a promising technology on the spectrum of quantum devices from specialized quantum experiments to universal quantum computers.

## Finding
It is the position of the community, as represented by participants of the National Science Foundation workshop on "Programmable Quantum Simulators," that investment in a national quantum simulator program is a high.
Attendees were charged to identify the scientific and community needs, opportunities, and significant challenges for quantum simulators over the next 2–5 years.
