# Efficient Z gates for quantum computing

## Method
For superconducting qubits, microwave pulses drive rotations around the Bloch sphere.

## Evaluation
For superconducting qubits, microwave pulses drive rotations around the Bloch sphere.
$Z$ gates can correct unitary rotation errors for weakly anharmonic qubits as an alternative to pulse-shaping techniques such as derivative removal by adiabatic gate (DRAG).

## Finding
Here we show how to best utilize these virtual $Z$ gates to both improve algorithms and correct pulse errors.
