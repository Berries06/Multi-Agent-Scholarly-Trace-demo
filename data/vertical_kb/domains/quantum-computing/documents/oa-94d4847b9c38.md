# Quantum algorithms: an overview

## Method
Abstract Quantum computers are designed to outperform standard computers by running quantum algorithms.

## Evaluation
Areas in which quantum algorithms can be applied include cryptography, search and optimisation, simulation of quantum systems and solving large systems of linear equations.

## Finding
Abstract Quantum computers are designed to outperform standard computers by running quantum algorithms.
