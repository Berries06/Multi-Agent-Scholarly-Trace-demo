# Variational quantum algorithms for nonlinear problems

## Method
The key concepts of the algorithm are demonstrated for the nonlinear Schr\"odinger equation as a canonical example.

## Evaluation
The key concepts of the algorithm are demonstrated for the nonlinear Schr\"odinger equation as a canonical example.

## Finding
We show that nonlinear problems including nonlinear partial differential equations can be efficiently solved by variational quantum computing.
We achieve this by utilizing multiple copies of variational quantum states to treat nonlinearities efficiently and by introducing tensor networks as a programming paradigm.
