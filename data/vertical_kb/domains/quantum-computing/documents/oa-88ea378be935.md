# Qubit teleportation between non-neighbouring nodes in a quantum network

## Method
The network uses three optically connected nodes based on solid-state spin qubits.

## Evaluation
Although many experimental demonstrations have been performed on different quantum network platforms4–10, moving beyond directly connected nodes has, so far, been hindered by the demanding requirements on the pre-shared.
The network uses three optically connected nodes based on solid-state spin qubits.

## Finding
We demonstrate that, once successful preparation of the teleporter is heralded, arbitrary qubit states can be teleported with fidelity above the classical bound, even with unit efficiency.
These results are enabled by key innovations in the qubit readout procedure, active memory qubit protection during entanglement generation and tailored heralding that reduces remote entanglement infidelities.
